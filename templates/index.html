A better first try

Give the model a clear view.

  1. Use one hand. Keep the whole hand inside the camera frame.
  2. Face the light. A plain background and even light help the landmark detector.
  3. Hold the sign. Wait for the next response before changing your pose.

This is an alphabet classifier, not a translator for continuous signing. A displayed label is a model prediction, not a guaranteed interpretation.
